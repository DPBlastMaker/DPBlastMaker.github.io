About DPBlast Host

DP Blast lets you create custom profile picture frames for any event, campaign, or movement.

How it works:

  • Creators upload a transparent PNG frame and get a shareable link
  • Anyone opens the link, uploads their profile photo, and positions it within the frame
  • Download the final composite image and set it as your profile picture
